# SOP-01: Inbound Receiving

Applies to all ambient inbound shipments at the dock.

## Check-in procedure
1. Verify the shipment ID against the carrier manifest before the trailer doors open.
2. Count pallets and compare to the bill-of-lading line count. Any shortage is
   noted on the manifest and photographed before unloading.
3. Stage pallets in the inbound hold area, never directly in pick aisles.

## Late shipments
1. A shipment is late when its ETA day passes with status still in_transit.
2. At 2 days late, the receiving lead calls the carrier for a revised ETA.
3. At 5 days late, escalate to T3: the inbound supervisor re-plans labor for
   the receiving shift and notifies the wave planner, because late receipts
   starve the next pick wave.

## Damaged goods
1. Damaged cartons are segregated to the damage cage and logged with the
   shipment ID, SKU, and unit count.
2. Never put away damaged units into pick slots.
